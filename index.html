BACKGROUND

About me

My name is Bricen Quebedeaux, and I am pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at Lamar University, with an expected graduation date of December 2026. My interests include software development, information technology support, computer hardware, and operating systems. I enjoy learning how technology works, developing programs that solve problems, and helping others understand technical issues. I am preparing for entry-level opportunities in software development and IT support, where I can continue learning and contribute to a team.

Throughout my coursework, I have studied programming, operating systems, computer architecture, networking, cybersecurity, and software engineering. These subjects have helped me understand both the software people use and the systems that support it. My projects include a Java Cache Simulator that explores computer memory behavior, a C++ Custom Command-Line Shell that demonstrates process creation and command execution, and a browser-based PC Troubleshooting Assistant developed with AI assistance. Each project has given me a different opportunity to practice problem-solving, testing, and technical documentation.

Outside the classroom, I work as a Sales Advisor at Best Buy, where I help customers compare technology products and understand their options. I have also assisted Geek Squad technicians with Windows troubleshooting, software issues, data transfers, computer diagnostics, and hardware upgrades such as RAM replacement. These experiences have strengthened my communication skills and taught me the importance of approaching technical problems carefully and explaining solutions in a way people can understand.

I have earned TestOut Desktop Pro, Network Pro, and Security Pro certifications. I want to build on that foundation through additional hands-on experience in software development, technical support, and systems administration. My professional goal is to find a role where I can combine technical knowledge with practical troubleshooting and continue improving as a Computer Science graduate. I value teamwork, patience, clear communication, and the willingness to keep working on a problem until I understand it.
